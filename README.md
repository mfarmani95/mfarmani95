## About
I am a Ph.D. student in Hydrology & Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Arizona, with a minor in Data Science. My research integrates physical hydrology, land surface modeling, and physics-guided machine learning to improve prediction of streamflow, soil moisture, snow, and evapotranspiration.

## Research Areas
- Hydrologic and land surface modeling with Noah-MP and RAPID
- Differentiable hydrology and physics-guided machine learning in PyTorch
- Precipitation forcing evaluation and hydroclimatic analysis
- Soil moisture, groundwater, recharge, and runoff processes
- Reproducible scientific workflows with Python, HPC, and geospatial data tools

## Selected Repositories
- **Noah-MP Modeling Experiments** – hydrologic and land-surface simulations for Salt–Verde basins
- **Differentiable Soil Solver** – PyTorch-based process components for soil moisture and water table dynamics
- **NeuralHydrology Experiments** – custom configurations and loss functions for streamflow prediction
- **Research Project Pages** – figures, summaries, and technical notes
